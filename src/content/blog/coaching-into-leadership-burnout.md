Over thirty years in senior leadership positions — across government ministries, international NGOs, and global consulting networks — I have watched hundreds of extraordinarily capable people hit an invisible wall.

They didn't fail because they lacked intellect, technical training, or dedication. Most often, they stalled because:
- The sheer friction of institutional bureaucracy drained their initial sense of mission.
- No one gave them the safe, confidential space to ask what they *actually* wanted next.
- The habits that made them successful in early management were actively obstructing their transition into strategic leadership.

### Working at Two Levels

That is the exact gap I coach into now through Red Thread.

#### 1. For Individuals
Maybe it is exhaustion that a weekend off cannot fix. Maybe your career made sense five years ago, but today feels completely foreign. Or perhaps you are stepping into a daunting executive post and need an experienced sounding board.

We work together — face-to-face, over secure video, or on WhatsApp — to unpack the root causes, strip away unhelpful patterns, and construct realistic, energising practices.

#### 2. For Teams and Organisations
In organisations undergoing restructuring, leadership turnover, or strategic pivots, exhaustion is contagious. Group coaching helps leadership teams move past defensive silos, realign with core purpose, and rediscover how to collaborate with genuine trust.

> *"Leadership and institutions are only ever as good as the people inside them, and those people need somewhere to reflect and grow."*

If you are navigating a transition in your career or organisation, I invite you to reach out for a complimentary 15-minute conversation to explore what working together could look like.
