At Red Thread we turn testimony into transformation. Using creative, people-centred methods, Red Thread enables individuals and groups to find their voice whether in search of personal development or the creation of a more just, equal society.

We work towards positive personal and social change through **creativity, coaching, and consulting**.

### The Threads That Bind Us

Red Thread works at the intersection of craft, art, writing, and social change. We use creative methods — rooted in South African storytelling and craftivism traditions — to amplify the voices of those too often left unheard, and to open new ways of confronting the challenges of an unequal society.

Alongside this, we offer consulting for change — particularly in the water sector — and build capacity for transformational leadership through coaching and mentoring.

> *"Change is stitched together one thread, one story, one voice at a time, whether through groups working together or individuals pursuing personal development needs."*

### Why Red Thread?

In ancient traditions across the world, the red thread represents the invisible, unbreakable connection between people destined to meet and learn from one another. In our work, the red thread is both metaphor and tangible material:

1. **A stitch of resistance**: Drawing from the arpillera textile traditions of Chile and South African community craft, needle and cloth hold testimonies that cannot easily be voiced through conventional channels.
2. **A line of dialogue**: Whether sitting with an individual grappling with burnout or facilitating a workshop of public sector executives, listening deeply creates the opening for honest reflection.
3. **A path to transformation**: We do not impose ready-made answers from outside. We unpack what is already present, bringing lived experience to the surface so that authentic change can take root.

We invite you to connect, converse, and create with us.
